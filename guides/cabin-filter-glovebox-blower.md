# Cabin (pollen) filter, glove box, fan motor V2 and final stage J126 (A1 / S1 8X)

**Applies to:** All A1 / S1 8X, LHD and RHD (the parts sit on the passenger side either way).

![Removal order behind the glove box: dash end cap, glove box, J431 and J518 on some cars, fan motor V2, then J126 out through the fan opening; the cabin filter is a separate shallow job through a slot cover with the glove box left in, and it also comes out before J126.](../diagrams/glovebox-access-order.svg)

## Cabin filter: the glove box stays in

The filter slides out of a slot under the passenger side of the dash. You don't need to remove the glove box.

1. Lay paper or a towel on the passenger footwell carpet. Old filters drop a lot of debris.
2. Under the glove box you'll find a slot cover held by two sliders. Push both sliders towards the middle.
3. Drop the cover and pull the filter straight out (it may come with or without its frame).
4. Vacuum the slot.
5. Slide the new filter in with its **airflow arrows** the right way. It has to seal all round: air must not be
   able to bypass it.
6. Refit the cover and push both sliders fully outwards. If a slider won't go to the end, the cover or the filter
   frame isn't seated.

## Glove box removal (needed for the fan and J126)

1. **If your glove box has the passenger airbag on/off key switch (E224):** disconnect the battery earth first and
   touch the door striker to discharge yourself before unplugging anything. Reconnect with nobody in the car.
2. Pop off the passenger-side dash end cap (the fuse cover) with a plastic trim wedge.
3. Open the glove box. There are 4 bolts inside.
4. Ease it out far enough to unplug its connector (lamp, and E224 if fitted).
5. Refitting: the bolts are only **1.5 Nm**, tightened in a cross pattern (1, 3, 2, 4). Make sure the connectors click.
   If the airbag light stays on afterwards, read and clear faults in VCDS 15.

## Fan motor V2 (Climatronic cars)

1. Glove box out.
2. Some cars have the headlight range control unit (J431) and the keyless entry/start unit (J518) mounted in front
   of the fan. Unbolt them and move them aside.
3. Unplug the fan, cut the cable tie and move the harness clear. **Careful:** the harness brackets clip onto small
   pins moulded into the fan housing. Snap one and you're buying a new fan.
4. Remove the fan's bolts. One is only reachable through the opening in the central air duct: you'll need an
   extension and a universal joint.
5. Pull the fan rearwards out of the housing. Hold it by the housing, **not the fan wheel** (it carries balance weights).

## Fan final stage J126 (Climatronic cars)

Cars with manual heating/A/C have a series resistor with overheating fuse (N24) in roughly the same place instead.

1. Fan motor out (above), cabin filter out.
2. Unplug J126 and undo its bolt. **The heat sink can be hot** if the fan has just been running.
3. Push J126 inwards and lift it out through the fan opening.
4. Refit at 1.5 Nm.
5. VCDS 08: clear faults → basic setting → output test of the fan.
