# Fuses by colour on a right-hand-drive A1 / S1 (8X)

The dash fuse holders on the 8X are behind the end caps at **both ends of the dashboard**. Fuse charts online and
in the owner's manual usually show a left-hand-drive car, and the rows are identified by the **colour of the
fuse holder row** (black, brown, ...) rather than by position. On a right-hand-drive car the holders swap sides,
so **go by colour and number, not by "left" or "right"**.

How to read it:
1. Pop off the end cap with a plastic wedge.
2. Find the row by its colour, then count the fuse number printed on the holder.
3. Check the owner's manual's fuse section for your build (the RHD charts are by colour too).

## Heater and A/C fuses we verified on an RHD S1

| Where (RHD) | Row | Fuse | Rating | Feeds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Driver's side dash end (holder C) | black | 2 | 40 A | Fresh-air blower: final stage J126, motor V2, heater control J65, X-contact relief relay J59 |
| Driver's side dash end | brown | 11 | 10 A | Heating / A/C (terminal 30 feed, Climatronic J255) |
| Passenger side dash end | brown | 2 | 10 A | A/C / heater control panel |

The common mistake: checking a small 10 A fuse for a dead fan. The fan's power is the **40 A black-row fuse**.

This table is only what we checked by hand. For anything else, use the chart for your car's build date and
equipment. The full list is in the *fuse assignment* current flow diagram.
